Working principle

Technology in service of artistic storytelling.

Creation, technical planning and operation are conceived as parts of the same system. Technology is selected and configured according to the space, the dramaturgy and the specific needs of each production.

A project may commission one specific service or combine different modules under a single technical direction.

The goal is not to impose an infrastructure, but to build precisely the system the work requires.

Technical product

Technical Rider · 16-Channel Immersive System

Complete specifications for setting up and operating an immersive system with 16 independent channels, prepared for local production, touring and adaptation to different spaces.

Deliverables

The rider may include

  • Signal architecture and distribution across 16 independent channels
  • Speaker layout plan
  • Loudspeaker angles and orientation
  • Multichannel D/A interface and conversion
  • Signal routing and matrix
  • Loudspeaker, amplification and cabling specifications
  • Ambisonics, decoding and spatialization
  • MIDI, OSC and synchronization where applicable
  • Electrical and technical infrastructure
  • Setup, alignment, testing and tuning
  • Required technical crew
  • Load-in, soundcheck, operation and load-out

Commercial terms

Built around the project.

Airfare and travel costs are quoted separately.
Accommodation, meals / per diem and local transportation are separate from professional fees.
Equipment transport, technical baggage, insurance and permits are quoted according to production requirements.
